Linear Programming Bounds & Theoretical Attack Limits

Standard empirical estimations $\hat{\lambda}_G = \sum \hat{f}_i / N$ artificially overestimate attack success ($\hat{\lambda}_N = 1$ flaw). Linear Programming constructs finite constraints to establish mathematically rigorous upper and lower bounds without shape assumptions.
